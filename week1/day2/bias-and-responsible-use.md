# Bias and Responsible AI Use

AI models can reflect biases present in their training data.

## What is Bias?
Systematic favoritism or unfair outcomes in AI-generated outputs.

**Example:**  
Stereotyped assumptions in generated text or decisions.

---

## Why Bias Occurs
- Skewed training data
- Historical inequalities
- Lack of contextual understanding

---

## Responsible Use Practices
- Review outputs critically
- Avoid sensitive decision-making
- Maintain human oversight

---

## Key Takeaway
Responsible AI use requires awareness, review, and accountability.

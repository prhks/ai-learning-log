# When NOT to Use AI

AI is powerful, but there are situations where it should be avoided.

## Do Not Use AI When:
- Working with security-critical systems
- Handling sensitive or regulated data
- Writing cryptographic or authentication logic
- Learning new fundamentals (languages, algorithms)
- Preparing for interviews or assessments

---

## Why This Matters
Overusing AI can:
- Weaken foundational skills
- Introduce security risks
- Create false confidence

---

## Key Takeaway
AI should accelerate expertise, not replace it.
